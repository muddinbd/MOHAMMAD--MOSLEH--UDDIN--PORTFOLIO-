## Process Improvement & SOPs

This section documents the standard operating procedures I use and have helped develop across treasury and payment operations. These SOPs exist because good operations should not depend on one person remembering everything — they should be written down clearly enough that anyone trained can follow them.

---

### SOP 001 — Daily Cash Position Management

**Purpose:** Maintain an accurate intraday view of liquidity across all accounts at all times.

**Frequency:** Daily — before 10:00 AM and after 4:00 PM

**Process Steps:**

```
Step 1 — Morning Position
  1.1  Log in to all banking portals and download overnight statements
  1.2  Record opening balance for each account in the daily cash tracker
  1.3  Identify any overnight transactions not yet processed
  1.4  Flag any balance below the minimum threshold for management attention
  1.5  Cross-check total position against prior-day closing figure

Step 2 — Intraday Monitoring
  2.1  Update balances after each wire received or sent
  2.2  Record all mobile wallet top-ups and payouts in real time
  2.3  Flag any unrecognised credit or debit immediately to line manager
  2.4  Ensure pre-funding is in place before large scheduled settlements

Step 3 — End of Day Close
  3.1  Download final statements from all banking portals
  3.2  Confirm all expected wires have arrived or been sent
  3.3  Record closing balance for each account
  3.4  Note any outstanding items for next-day follow-up
  3.5  Send daily cash summary to management by 5:00 PM
```

**Controls:**
- Dual review required for positions above threshold
- Any movement of funds requires written authorisation
- All entries logged with timestamp and reference number

---

### SOP 002 — Monthly Bank Reconciliation

**Purpose:** Ensure that the company's internal records match the official bank statements for every account at month-end.

**Frequency:** Monthly — completed within 3 business days of month-end

**Process Steps:**

```
Step 1 — Gather Documents
  1.1  Download official bank statements for all accounts
  1.2  Export transaction records from internal system
  1.3  Confirm all transactions have been entered to the ledger

Step 2 — Statement Matching
  2.1  Open the reconciliation template
  2.2  Enter statement opening balance
  2.3  Match each bank entry to an internal ledger entry
  2.4  Mark matched items as cleared
  2.5  List unmatched items in the reconciling items section

Step 3 — Investigate Differences
  3.1  For each unmatched item, identify the cause:
       - Timing difference (entry in one system, pending in other)
       - Missing entry (not yet recorded in ledger)
       - Error (incorrect amount or account)
  3.2  For timing differences: note expected clearing date
  3.3  For missing entries: create journal entry with supporting document
  3.4  For errors: prepare correction with manager approval

Step 4 — Sign-Off
  4.1  Confirm adjusted balance equals bank statement balance
  4.2  Record reconciliation discrepancy (target: zero)
  4.3  Attach all supporting documents to the completed reconciliation
  4.4  Submit to manager for review and sign-off
  4.5  File completed reconciliation in the monthly folder

Step 5 — Reporting
  5.1  Record reconciliation status in the monthly summary tracker
  5.2  Note any open items with estimated resolution date
  5.3  Flag any recurring discrepancy pattern for process review
```

**Controls:**
- Reconciliation must be reviewed by a second person before sign-off
- Any discrepancy above threshold must be escalated same day
- All reconciliations retained for minimum 7 years

**Quality Standard:** Target discrepancy — zero. Acceptable rounding tolerance — less than 0.05 local currency units.

### SOP 003 — Digital Wallet Reconciliation (Mobile Money Platforms)

**Purpose:** Reconcile the balance and transaction history of each digital wallet account against internal payout records.

**Frequency:** Monthly (daily spot-checks recommended for high-volume wallets)

**Process Steps:**

```
Step 1 — Collect Wallet Reports
  1.1  Log in to each wallet platform and export the monthly statement
  1.2  Record: opening balance, total payouts, total top-ups, closing balance
  1.3  Note any platform-generated fees or charges

Step 2 — Match to Internal Records
  2.1  Total all payout entries in internal system for the wallet
  2.2  Total all top-up entries in internal system for the wallet
  2.3  Calculate: Opening + Top-ups - Payouts - Fees = Expected Closing
  2.4  Compare expected closing to platform-reported closing

Step 3 — Investigate Differences
  3.1  Pull transaction-level detail for any unmatched amount
  3.2  Common causes to check:
       - Payout processed on platform but not logged internally
       - Top-up sent but not reflected in platform yet
       - Fee charged by platform not recorded in system
       - Cancelled transaction not reversed in one system

Step 4 — Resolve and Document
  4.1  Correct any missing or incorrect entries in the internal system
  4.2  Note any platform-side issues and follow up with provider
  4.3  Document final reconciliation status and discrepancy (if any)
  4.4  Obtain manager sign-off

Step 5 — Escalation
  5.1  Any discrepancy above threshold: escalate to senior management
  5.2  Any unexplained credit: flag to compliance immediately
  5.3  Recurring platform errors: document and raise with provider formally
```

### SOP 004 — Intercompany Fund Transfer

**Purpose:** Ensure all intercompany fund movements are authorised, documented, and reconciled correctly on both sides of the transfer.

**Frequency:** As required — typically multiple times per month

**Process Steps:**

```
Step 1 — Initiation
  1.1  Receive or identify the funding request in writing
  1.2  Confirm: amount, receiving entity, required value date, bank details
  1.3  Verify sufficient balance in sending account
  1.4  Obtain written authorisation from approved signatory

Step 2 — Execution
  2.1  Log in to banking portal and initiate transfer
  2.2  Enter all details carefully — amount, beneficiary, reference
  2.3  Record the payment reference number
  2.4  Take a screenshot or download the confirmation
  2.5  Notify receiving entity that transfer has been sent

Step 3 — Confirmation
  3.1  Follow up with receiving entity to confirm funds arrived
  3.2  Record the date and time of confirmed receipt
  3.3  Match the transfer in both the sending and receiving ledgers

Step 4 — Documentation
  4.1  File the authorisation, payment confirmation, and receipt confirmation
  4.2  Record in the intercompany transfer log:
       - Date initiated / Date received
       - Amount and currency
       - Sending bank and receiving bank
       - Reference numbers (both sides)
       - Authorised by / Executed by
  4.3  Include in month-end intercompany reconciliation

Step 5 — Reconciliation
  5.1  At month-end, reconcile all intercompany balances
  5.2  Confirm that every transfer sent matches a receipt on the other side
  5.3  Investigate any timing differences and document expected clearing
```

**Controls:**
- No transfer may be initiated without written authorisation
- Transfers above threshold require dual approval
- All transfers documented with supporting evidence before month-end close

### SOP 005 — Month-End Close Procedure

**Purpose:** Complete all financial recording, reconciliation, and reporting by the agreed close deadline.

**Frequency:** Monthly — typically within 3–5 business days of month-end

**Checklist:**

```
PRE-CLOSE (Last day of month)
  [ ]  All transactions for the month entered in the system
  [ ]  All pending wires and payments followed up
  [ ]  Daily cash position updated and confirmed
  [ ]  All wallet top-ups and payouts recorded

CLOSE — DAY 1
  [ ]  Download all bank statements
  [ ]  Complete bank reconciliation for each account
  [ ]  Complete wallet reconciliation for each platform
  [ ]  Reconcile branch fund movements

CLOSE — DAY 2
  [ ]  Reconcile intercompany accounts (sent vs received)
  [ ]  Review all open items from prior month — confirm cleared or escalate
  [ ]  Prepare variance analysis (budget vs actual if applicable)
  [ ]  Prepare draft financial summary for management

CLOSE — DAY 3
  [ ]  Manager review of all reconciliations
  [ ]  Obtain sign-off on completed reconciliations
  [ ]  Finalise monthly financial performance report
  [ ]  Submit report to senior management

POST-CLOSE
  [ ]  File all supporting documents in the monthly folder
  [ ]  Note any open items and assign resolution owners
  [ ]  Update the reconciliation status tracker
  [ ]  Begin preparation for following month
```

**Quality Targets:**
- All bank reconciliations: zero discrepancy
- All wallet reconciliations: discrepancy less than 0.05 local currency units
- Report delivery: within 5 business days of month-end

### SOP 006 — eWallet Partner SOA Verification

**Purpose:** Verify the Statement of Account received from settlement partners against internal transaction records to ensure correct billing and accurate net settlement.

**Frequency:** Monthly — upon receipt of partner SOA

**Process Steps:**

```
Step 1 — Receive SOA
  1.1  Download or receive the SOA from the partner system
  1.2  Note: opening balance, total sends, total cancels, closing balance
  1.3  Note any fees, adjustments, or disputes listed

Step 2 — Transaction-Level Verification
  2.1  Export all send transactions from internal records for the period
  2.2  Match each send to the corresponding SOA entry by:
       - Transaction reference number
       - Amount and currency
       - Date and corridor
  2.3  Mark each matched item as verified
  2.4  List all unmatched items for investigation

Step 3 — Fee Verification
  3.1  Calculate expected fees: (total send volume × agreed fee rate)
  3.2  Compare to fees shown on SOA
  3.3  Flag any discrepancy for follow-up with partner

Step 4 — Cancellation Matching
  4.1  List all cancellations on the SOA
  4.2  Confirm each cancellation has a corresponding send that was cancelled
  4.3  Verify credit amount matches original send amount

Step 5 — Close and Report
  5.1  Calculate: Opening + Net Sends - Net Cancels - Fees = Expected Closing
  5.2  Compare to SOA closing balance
  5.3  If difference exists: document and escalate to partner for resolution
  5.4  If zero difference: sign off and file
  5.5  Record in monthly summary: transactions verified, fees confirmed, discrepancy
```

## Process Improvement — What I Changed and Why

One of the things I am most proud of in this role is not just doing the work but making the work easier for everyone. Here are three process improvements I introduced:

---

**1. Automated Monthly Dashboard**

The problem: every month-end involved manually copying figures from four different region reports into a summary, which took hours and introduced risk of copy errors.

What I built: a linked Excel dashboard that pulls directly from each regional report and auto-calculates the consolidated summary, variance analysis, and reconciliation status.

Result: 40% reduction in month-end processing time. Zero copy errors. Management gets the summary faster.

---

**2. Reconciliation Status Tracker**

The problem: it was not easy to see at a glance which accounts had been reconciled and which were still outstanding during the close period.

What I introduced: a simple shared tracker showing every account, the reconciliation status, the discrepancy (if any), and the sign-off date. Updated in real time during close.

Result: managers can see close progress without asking. Open items are visible immediately. Nothing gets missed.

---

**3. Intercompany Transfer Log**

The problem: intercompany transfers were confirmed by email but there was no single place to see all movements for the month — which made reconciliation slow and the audit trail fragmented.

What I created: a structured monthly log capturing every transfer with: date initiated, date received, amount, sending account, receiving entity, reference numbers, authorised by, and confirmed by.

Result: month-end intercompany reconciliation now takes less than 30 minutes instead of half a day. Audit support is immediate.
